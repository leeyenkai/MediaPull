# Audit 2026-11-21, round 13: metadata edits and Edit/Frame

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 12 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md) to [12](2026-11-21-12.md)), with fresh auditors who first
verified round 12's fixes. Totals: 0 critical, 6 majors, 9 minors.

Two of the majors were regressions from round 12, and both are answered by stating the rule more simply.
- A13-1 came from A12-m3, where a photo led only its clip by name but listings still let it lead other photos. The
  rule is now the same everywhere: a photo leads its clip only, and Undo refuses a file only where it would join a unit
  with another shot.
- C13-1 came from C12-1, where surround was read by labels even when it had none. Labels are now used only when every
  channel has a place.

## Minors

Fixed:
- A13-m1: a metadata edit of a still no longer rewrites a JPEG of its name, since the JPEG is its own item.
- A13-m2: Merge no longer reports false failures from an `.xmp` listed as its own companion.
- B13-m1: an export is fitted to its size setting's own box, so Long Edge 1080 gives 1080
  (`testB13LongEdge1080Is1080`, `testB13EverySizeSettingGivesTheSizeItAsks`).
- C13 minor 1: the waveform draws sounds up to about 11 kHz at their level (`testC13WaveformShowsHighSounds`).
- C13 minor 2: the waveform streams its samples, so memory stays small whatever the length
  (`testC13WaveformOfALongFile`).
- C13 minor 4, in part:
  - Leftover temporary files are removed only when a day old, so a second MediaPull never takes a running export's
    files.
  - D-130's wording now says more than two channels.

Recorded and left (minor, nothing of the original lost):
- Moving two photos of one name together numbers the second in the destination. Placing keeps shots of one name
  apart; Undo puts both back as they were.
- C13 minor 3: re-encoding surround writes its sound as float to the temporary folder first, about 4 GB per hour of
  5.1. A full disk fails the export cleanly.
- C13 minor 4: surround exported as MP3 is mixed to stereo (MP3 holds two channels), and the Export tab does not say so.
- Not counted:
  - The system's own exporter treats the last of six or more discrete channels as the LFE (low-passed). MediaPull's
    export now matches it.
  - One unrepeated failure of `testA11UndoOfReplaceOfALivePhotosStillPutsTheOriginalBack` on a freshly attached image
    (passed 18 times after).
