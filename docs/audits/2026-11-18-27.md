# Audit 2026-11-18, round 27 (Save As, D-108, round 4)

The fourth audit of Save As, at commit 4139480, after round 26 rewrote rule 4 (entry folders by name alone). Two
independent auditors: A the Instagram side and its interaction with Automatic downloads, B videos, galleries, Download
All As… and the tests. A ran the real bridge's `tracker_for` and `KnownTracker` with the bundled Python. **No critical and
no major finding: the first of the two clean rounds AUDIT.md asks for.** Under the closing-round rule, only wording, spec
and test changes were made; minors that would change behaviour are recorded.

## Unproven

- Stand-in early stop counts files, not posts; an own post does not reset the run; it limits tray and saved items. No
  current step depends on these.
- An entry name equal by case alone (a post code and a username), or an account named like a type folder inside a
  parent that is an account folder.
- `id` vs `post_id` value collisions on Pillowfort-like sites; an entry name that is a symlink made by the person.
- The D-78 spec question carried from round 25.

## Checked and fine

Every Instagram Save As places files only in the chosen or entry folder; gone folders are never made again; no memory
outside the remembered-folder exception, which behaves as an Update; the engine lists carry the folder's index; Retry and
Resume keep `exact`, follow renames and never stop at their own items (the resumed Reels steps fail without the fixes);
videos and galleries go straight in or into `<name> (<site>)` with their side files; no archive writes; reruns add
nothing; Automatic paths unchanged; no egress change.
