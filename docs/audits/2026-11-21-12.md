# Audit 2026-11-21, round 12: metadata edits and Edit/Frame

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 11 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md) to [11](2026-11-21-11.md)), with fresh auditors who first
verified round 11's fixes. Totals: 0 critical, 6 majors, 13 minors.

Every round-11 fix held. The new majors are answered by two simpler rules, written into D-120 and D-127:
- A unit moves, is copied or goes to the Trash whole, or not at all.
- A Replace keeps a file's place in its shot.

The photo major also closes with one rule, added to D-128: the pictures a recipe draws from follow MediaPull's moves
everywhere.

## Minors

Fixed:
- A12-m1: a clip whose photo failed to import never joins another shot of its name (`testA12ClipWhosePhotoFailedNeverJoinsAnotherShotOfItsName`).
- A12-m2: saves into a folder on an exFAT or FAT drive work (`testA12ProbeWebSaveOntoExFAT`, on an exFAT image).
- A12-m3: two photos of one name are two items in the journal (`testA12TwoPhotosOfOneNameAreTwoItemsInTheJournal`).
- B12-m1: "Keep the location" off also removes IPTC's content location and the extension's locations.
- B12-m2: crops of a fixed shape are exact, so a 4:5 crop exported as a post is 1080 × 1350 (`testB12SquareCropExportsSquare`, `…FourByFivePostIs1080By1350`).
- B12-m3: text keeps the same share of the canvas at every size, because the optical size is fixed (`testB12TextWidthIsTheSameShareOfTheCanvasAtEverySize`).
- B12-m4: a transparent PNG set to export as JPEG shows white on the canvas, as it is written.
- B12-m5: an audio recipe's artwork follows a MediaPull move (`testB12AudioArtworkFollowsAMovedPicture`).
- C12-3: the waveform peaks across channels, so out-of-phase stereo is drawn (`testC12WaveformOfOppositeChannels`).
- C12-4: temporary sound, waveform and cut files a quit left are removed at the next launch.

Recorded and left (minor, nothing of the original lost):
- B12-m6: Export Selected does not say when a file other than the open one is drawn in the system font.
- C12-1 remainder: 7.1's rear pair has no place in the AAC encoder's 7.1, so it is silent in a re-encode; the other six channels stay in place.
- C12-2: lossless cuts of MP3 and M4A take in about 25 to 40 ms of sound around each part, so several parts drift. Packet-aligned cuts are a later change; encoded formats are exact.
- Noted by the auditor and not counted: a Replace that changes a file's extension beside a same-stem MOV can form a new unit.
