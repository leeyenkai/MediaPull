### Why round 4 still found majors

- The stand-in bridge was written from the design, not from gallery-dl: it said "complete" whenever its list ended, so the
  self-test proved the Swift bookkeeping but not the signal it depends on (A4-M1). Cause category 5.
- Closures were checked at the moment of the click, not for the life of a queued job (A4-M5/C4-M1), and for one pane, not
  every pane with the same action (B4-M2). Cause category 2.
- Requirements in older spec sections that 1.8 newly depends on had never been built (C4-M4, SPEC 23) or were classified
  too broadly (C4-M3). Cause category 1.
