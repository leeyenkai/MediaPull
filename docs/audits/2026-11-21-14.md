# Audit 2026-11-21, round 14: metadata edits and Edit/Frame (last round)

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 13 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md) to [13](2026-11-21-13.md)), with fresh auditors who
first verified round 13's fixes. Totals: 0 critical, 8 majors, 9 minors. On the person's instruction this is the last
round. It ends with these fixes and a final sweep rather than two clean rounds, so the exit rule in
[AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md) is not met; the open minors are listed below.

Three of the majors came from round 13's rule that a photo leads only its clip. Following the protocol, that rule was
withdrawn rather than patched:
- Units are grouped as they were before round 12: a Live Photo's still leads its other photos and its clip.
- The journal now records which files went with which item, so Undo and Redo take each item back whole, as it was
  moved, and never work its files out again from their names. Older journals are still grouped by name.
- Undo and Redo use the same stem rule as placing: a name another shot of the stem holds is taken. Files the same Undo
  or Redo puts back are never counted.
- A file's `.xmp` and `.aae` go with it only while no other shot of its stem stays.

## Minors

Fixed:
- B14-m1: corrections no longer depend on a history of moves.
- B14-m2: an SVG logo, which opens but cannot be drawn, is now said and refused at export. The panel offers only
  pictures the Mac can draw.
- B14-m3: the save at quit takes the stored picture path for a picture no longer at the window's name.
- C14 minor 1: waveform peaks are now taken every 64 samples, so short clips keep their resolution.
- C14 minor 3: FLAC and Apple Lossless keep a 16-bit source at 16 bits.
- C14 minor 2: D-130 now says a volume change re-encodes the picture.
- C14 minor 4: D-130 now says 7.1's rear pair is folded into the side surrounds.

Recorded and left (minor, nothing of the original lost):
- A copied unit with a member locked after the copy: Undo removes the lead copy and leaves the locked copy (the
  originals are untouched).
- A `.xmp` locked since a change is reported and left apart from its photo.
- `foldersRemoved` reads trash and folder steps on the main actor.
- A 32-bit float source exported to FLAC or Apple Lossless becomes 24-bit integer (anything over full scale is clipped),
  and the note does not say so for float.
- Sound of more than eight channels (Atmos with height pairs, higher-order ambisonics) cannot be re-encoded, as with the
  system's own exporter; the reason given is the system's.
- The last of six or more channels kept by number is the encoder's LFE, so it is low-passed, as with the system's own
  exporter.
