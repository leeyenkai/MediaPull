# Audit 2026-11-21, round 5: metadata edits and Edit/Frame

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 4 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md), [2](2026-11-21-2.md), [3](2026-11-21-3.md),
[4](2026-11-21-4.md)), with fresh auditors who first verified round 4's fixes. Totals: 0 critical, 4 majors, 21 minors.
Video and audio had no major for the second round running.

Two of the photo majors came from round 4's own fixes (B4-2 restoring a recipe's identity, B4-m3's cache). Under the
protocol's rule against patching a patch, they are answered by a simpler rule written into D-127: the store alone says
which file a recipe belongs to, callers change only the edits, and recipes are never deleted (Revert keeps one blank,
so ⌘Z brings the edits back wherever the file has gone).

## Majors

| ID | Finding | Fix | Proved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A5-1 | An edit threw away another app's `.xmp` that ImageIO cannot read (an old root element, a bare RDF root, UTF-16) and wrote a new one from empty | Such a `.xmp` holding anything is refused with the reason, never written over | `testAnXMPImageIOCannotReadIsNeverWrittenOverFromEmpty` |
| A5-2 | Round 4's A4-2 copied corrections over a live copy that holds the same content (duplicates rated alike are byte-identical) | Held faces take the edited file's corrections only when no other file holds that content (R-4506) | `testAnEditNeverOverwritesTheCorrectionsOfACopyWithTheSameContent` |
| B5-1 | ⌘Z after a Finder rename or a move lost the edits for good, or gave them to a newcomer at the old name (B4-2 cleared the identity) | The store keeps a held recipe's identity; Undo and Redo save by id; Revert keeps a blank recipe (D-127 amended) | `testUndoAfterAFinderRenameFollowsTheFileAndGivesANewcomerNothing`; the auditor's reproductions now pass |
| B5-2 | A Finder rename within ten seconds of a lookup was not followed, and edits made then hid the earlier ones (B4-m3's cache) | References are cached per recipe; one found away for the first time is resolved at once | `testAFinderRenameIsFollowedAtOnceAfterALookup` |

## Minors

Fixed: A5-3 (Redo makes a `.xmp` again only beside its photo), A5-4 (the lock is checked before anything is made), A5-5
(Undo and Redo list each folder once and check the lead and its `.xmp`, as the edit does), A5-6 (a rejected mark another
app set since is not written over), A5-7 (an edit records the photos it changed, a unit being one, so History counts
them as the edit did); B5-3 (⌘Z matched by path no longer gives one file's recipe to another: only the same id is
restored on the canvas), B5-m1 (one file's export is named by the file), B5-m2 (at quit a recipe keeps the identity the
store last wrote), B5-m3 (tile sizes are not copied into exports), B5-m4 (a recipe made during a long change goes with its
file); C5-1 (a BMP or GIF cover becomes PNG; the BMP decoder is added to the build), C5-2 (the sound's fade-out ends where
the sound does), C5-3 (Save Frame past the picture's end saves the last picture), C5-4 (the refusal says the parts kept
have no picture, and Play Result shows it).

Recorded and left (minor, no file lost): A5-3's newcomer case (a different photo given the name of one whose `.xmp`
Undo sent to the Trash gets that `.xmp` back on Redo; only a photo's presence is checked), C5-5 (a 7.1 source becomes
7 channels in AAC and Apple Lossless, whose encoders do not take the standard 7.1 layout; FLAC, WAV and AIFF keep 8).
