# Audit 2026-11-21, round 11: metadata edits and Edit/Frame

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 10 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md) to [10](2026-11-21-10.md)), with fresh auditors who first
verified round 10's fixes. Totals: 0 critical, 7 majors, 14 minors.

The shot rule (D-120) now holds through Replace, Redo of copies, web saves and renames across folders. A recipe open in
a window is never given a newcomer's identity, and every window is told about a change by recipe id. The sound of a video
at another speed is now rendered by the same live time-stretch Play Result uses, written out buffer by buffer. That one
change replaces round 10's offline stretch and closes five minors at once.

## Minors

Fixed:
- B11-3: windows open the file there again after a Replace, its Undo and its Redo, never following it into hiding (`replacedPaths`).
- B11-4: the canvas says Export waits for a missing picture.
- B11-5: a video export with a missing picture layer is refused (`testB11VideoExportWithAMissingPictureLayerIsRefused`).
- B11-6: the Media pane and its selection follow a rename the store followed.
- B11-7: the Border slider keeps the extra at the bottom.
- C11-1 to C11-5: the sound at another speed is read through a mix output with the spectral time-stretch (D-130 amended).
  - 5.1 and other surround sound keeps its channels and pitch (`testC11FiveOneAtAnotherSpeedKeepsPitchAndChannels`).
  - Bursts land within 20 ms at every speed (`testC11ExportedBurstsAreOnTimeAtEverySpeed`), and the end is kept (`testC11ToneRunsToTheEndAtEverySpeed`).
  - Cancel stops it at once (`testC11CancelDuringTheSoundRenderStopsPromptly`).
  - Memory stays small, the temporary file is removed when anything throws, and a reader failure is reported.
- C11-6: a part shorter than one tick at the speed exports without an empty sound track (`testC11TinyPartAtSpeed`).

Recorded and left (minor, nothing lost):
- A11-6: Undo is refused beside a same-stem file that was there all along. Safe: said, nothing moved.
- A11-7: Redo of a copy inside an account folder restores identity keys in the copy's metadata file.
- A11-8: a clip imported after its photo, in a later import, is numbered instead of joining it.
- C11-4 remainder: the temporary sound file is float, about 0.7 GB per hour of stereo at normal length.
