# Audit 2026-11-21, round 10: metadata edits and Edit/Frame

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 9 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md) to [9](2026-11-21-9.md)), with fresh auditors who first
verified round 9's fixes. Totals: 0 critical, 7 majors, 11 minors.

Four of the metadata majors are one gap: round 9's rule that a name another shot holds is taken (D-120) was enforced
where Organise plans names but not wherever else MediaPull places or puts back a file. It now is: batch renames
(within one rename too), placing (imports, conversions, saves, exports), and Undo, Redo and putting back from the Trash.

## Minors

Fixed: A10-m1 in part (a plan lists each folder once for the shot rule; the flatten of 3,000 files is back to its
earlier time); B10-3 (a Replace of the open file opens the new file as itself), B10-4 (an export with a missing picture
layer is refused, and copied settings follow moved pictures), B10-5 (a rounded photo's shadow is rounded), B10-6 (a drop
onto the Media pane that opens nothing says why); C10-2 (AAC from rates it does not take is resampled by name), C10-3
(normalised AAC stays at 44.1 kHz), C10-4 (Save Frame in an edit's last frame saves the picture shown).

Recorded and left (minor, nothing lost): A10-m1's remainder (planning a flatten of thousands of files still lists
companions per file, as A7-8), A10-m2 (a single rename in a folder of 20,000 files plans on the main actor, about 0.2
s), B10-7 (written into D-127: Locate across disks is not a MediaPull move), C10-5 (FLAC refuses more than eight
channels and AAC, Apple Lossless and MP3 change unusual layouts without saying so).
