## The person's decision: saved posts remember deletions (D-106)

Round 21 wrote down earlier versions' behaviour for saved posts (D-105: no memory). The person decided otherwise: by
default a pull of saved posts skips what its Saved posts folders remember, so a post deleted from Saved posts is not
fetched again, and a setting under Instagram turns this off. The memory is kept in Application Support (`SavedPosts/`),
apart from the account folders. `test_saved_posts_remember_what_was_deleted` (bridge) and
`testKnownListsGoUnderTheBridgesKeys` (the `saved_known_ids` key) cover it; a signed-in saved-posts pull is Person.
