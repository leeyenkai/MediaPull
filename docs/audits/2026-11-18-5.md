## Why majors keep appearing

This round's majors share one cause with round 4's: a closure checked at one place or one moment, not for every path.
The user-id check was proved against the stand-in, not the engine (the stand-in was again written from the design). The
folder-following closure covered files of one pull but not documents, other accounts' posts or scanned saves, and Finder
renames but not MediaPull's own. Saved links were fixed in the Downloads field but not in the two other ways to open it.
The journal guard covered merge but not the other plans.

Changes made so the next round can see these: the stand-in's events are checked against the bridge by a bridge test of
the real lookup; folder following is decided by record id in one place (`currentFolder(of:)`); the journal refuses any
plan whose root went away, for every plan; `check.sh` runs the offline update self-test; the self-test cases now reach
past the early stop and rename through MediaPull.
