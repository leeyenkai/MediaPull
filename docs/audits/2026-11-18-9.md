## Why majors still appear, and what changed

Round 9 found more majors than round 8, five in Undo. Rounds 6 to 8 each tried to make Undo follow renamed folders, a
convenience R-3806 never asked for, and each attempt opened new edges (path rewriting, then references that do not
survive a disk reconnect). This round removes following altogether: Undo and Redo act only on the same things in the
same places and report the rest, which is what R-3806 says. The checks are by disk identity, which the auditors' probes
showed survives reconnecting. Account folder records were changed the same way: they follow their folder itself instead
of guessing by path.
