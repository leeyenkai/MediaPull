# Audit 2026-11-18, round 26 (Save As, D-108, round 3)

The third audit of Save As, at commit b11a3e1. Two independent auditors: A the Instagram side and its interaction with
Automatic downloads, B videos, galleries, Download All As… and the tests. A ran the real bridge's `tracker_for`; B ran
`item_key` and `item_meta` and read gallery-dl 1.32.19's extractors. Every finding was checked against the code.

## Why rule 4 changed

Three majors (B26-M1, B26-M2 and, with B26-M4, the way an entry's folder decided its walk) came from Download All As…
finding entry folders by what they held, the A25-M4 fix included: a post's or a tag's folder filled with one account's
media looked like that account's folder. As AUDIT.md asks, D-108 rule 4 was rewritten instead of patched again: each
entry's folder is named after the entry (`<account>`, `#<tag>`, the post's code, `Saved posts`, `<channel or user>
(<site>)`), found by that name alone and reused whenever it is there; an account entry walks its whole listing.

## Unproven (carried)

- Save As copies can reach Automatic memory indirectly when a later Update of a remembered folder records what a Save As
  put there (a spec question, like the D-78 one carried from round 25).
- A folder that becomes remembered between a Save As job's runs switches the job to the remembered branch.
- A remembered chosen folder moved to another volume may not be followed by its bookmark.
- Pillowfort and SubscribeStar key files by their own id while metadata files give the post id: re-downloaded, dropped
  as copies.
- Plain-layout and converted re-downloads may not be byte-identical; long `<name> (<site>)` names lose the suffix.

## Checked and fine (A and B)

Own media into the chosen folder's type folders and everything else straight in; nothing recreated; no memory written
outside the remembered-folder exception, which keeps D-88, places and history as an Update does; the tray and saved posts
read against the folder's list; Retry keeps `exact` and `perSource`; videos and gallery files stay in their folders with
their side files; no archive writes; keys for num 0, missing numbers and the `id` fallback agree with `item_key`;
Automatic paths unchanged; no egress change.
