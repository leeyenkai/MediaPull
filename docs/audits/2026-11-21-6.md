# Audit 2026-11-21, round 6: metadata edits and Edit/Frame

Same scope and split as rounds 1 to 5 ([1](2026-11-21-1.md) to [5](2026-11-21-5.md)), with fresh auditors who first
verified round 5's fixes. Totals: 2 critical, 5 majors, 16 minors. The criticals were found in video, after two rounds
there without a major: both crash the app inside AVFoundation and are proved by tests that crash with the old code.

## Critical

| ID | Finding | Fix | Proved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| C6-1 | Fades longer together than the clip (or its sound, since C5-2) overlapped their ramps and crashed export and Play Result | Fades longer than their media are shortened alike so they meet; each runs over its own media | `testFadesLongerThanTheMediaNeverStopAnExportOrPlayback` |
| C6-2 | A lossless export whose kept parts have no sound crashed the exporter (an empty sound track) | No sound track is written when no kept part has sound | `testKeptPartsWithoutSoundAreWrittenWithoutASoundTrack` |

## Majors

| ID | Finding | Fix | Proved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| A6-1 | Redo of an older `.xmp` change wrote over a newer edit of the same field | A Redo requires a field the `.xmp` did not hold before to be absent now | `testRedoOfAnOlderXMPChangeLeavesANewerOne` |
| A6-2 | Undo of a move or copy split a unit when its `.xmp` changed after the move (R-4206) | Edit files follow their photo; one made after the move goes back with it; an edited copy stays whole (D-120 amended) | `testUndoOfAMoveKeepsTheXMPWithItsPhotoAndAnEditedCopyStaysWhole` |
| A6-3 | A name given after an edit was lost on Undo then Redo when a duplicate held the earlier content (from A5-2) | Corrections are carried only when neither content is held by another file | `testANameGivenAfterAnEditSurvivesUndoAndRedoWithADuplicate` |
| B6-1 | Edits of a photo renamed in the Finder were lost if MediaPull rated it in place or moved it to another disk before Edit/Frame looked it up | Finder renames are followed before MediaPull moves or rewrites files (`awayIDs` relocates first; metadata edits ask it) | `testAFinderRenameIsFollowedBeforeMediaPullRewritesTheFile` |
| B6-2 | Two windows on one file: the second to change threw away the first's edits | A window shows a recipe another window saved, unless it has a change of its own unsaved | code (`savedHere`, `recipeSavedElsewhere`) |

## Minors

Fixed: A6-4 (an empty packet, white space or an empty xpacket wrapper is nothing; an unreadable `.xmp` is named in the
reason), A6-5 (an Undo step refused for a locked photo is tried again once it is unlocked), A6-7 (one folder listing
per batch before an edit begins); B6-m1 (a recipe first written at quit takes a reference), B6-m2 (export settings
alone are kept and opened with the file; only edited recipes are marked), B6-m3 (a lookup compares the file's
reference instead of checking every kept recipe), B6-m4 (the open recipe is reloaded by its id, following a Finder
rename; a new recipe knows its file from the start), B6-m5 (a Replace leaves the recipe with the original, written into
D-127), B6-m6 (a missing font face is said); C6-3 (each cover is kept or turned into PNG on its own, by what the output
holds; the TIFF decoder is added), C6-4 (fade-ins start where the sound or picture starts), C6-5 (a cover's description
is not taken as the title), C6-6 (Play Result's refusal is shown only for the file still open, worded for playing).

Recorded and left (minor, no file lost): A6-6 (after Forget Imports, Import New brings an edited photo in again, as its
size changed; duplicates only).

## Checked and fine (from the reports)

Round 5's fixes verified: the refusal of an unreadable `.xmp`, a copy's corrections kept on the forward edit, the
per-Undo listing and lead rule for RAW pairs, ProRAW and HEIC+JPG+MOV, the Redo remake needing its photo, rejected
marks in read-backs, `photos` counts with old journals; the store's identity rule traced through edit, paste, revert,
undo and redo, quit, moves, Finder renames, metadata edits, two cards with one name and cross-disk moves; the audio
stream and cover codecs, the BMP decoder, the sound's fade end, the last frame on an MP4 with B-frames and an edit
list. Edits, Undo and Redo on real FAT32 and exFAT images leave no hidden names; originals are never written.
