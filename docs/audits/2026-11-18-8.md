## Why majors still appear, and what changed this round

Both journal majors came from following folders by path, which rounds 6 and 7 each patched. This round removed that
mechanism instead of patching it again: changes record references to what they touched, and the auditors' probe scenarios
from rounds 6 to 8 are package tests. The account-id fallback, which two rounds patched, is removed for the same reason:
its premise did not hold for 1.7 data. The other majors were siblings of round-7 closures (a second way into the
Downloads pane, a second name field in the editor).
